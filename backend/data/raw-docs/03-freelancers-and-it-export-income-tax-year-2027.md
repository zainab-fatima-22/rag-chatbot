# Pakistan Freelancers and IT Export Income — Tax Year 2027

## Important distinction

“Freelancer” is not by itself a single tax rate or tax category. The correct treatment depends on the nature and source of the income.

A person providing services to foreign clients may have export-of-services income. Eligible export proceeds from computer software, IT services, or IT-enabled services can fall under section 154A and the relevant withholding-tax regime. A freelancer earning local business or professional income should not automatically be given the section 154A export rate.

## Section 154A — eligible IT and IT-enabled export services

The FBR withholding-tax framework provides a reduced rate for export proceeds of computer software, IT services, or IT-enabled services by persons registered with the Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB).

For the current framework, the commonly applicable section 154A rates are:

- **0.25% of export proceeds** for eligible computer software, IT services, or IT-enabled services by a person registered with PSEB.
- **1% of export proceeds** for the other section 154A export-services case.

The exact tax treatment depends on the statutory conditions and the taxpayer's circumstances. Do not tell a user that every freelancer automatically pays 0.25%.

## Practical classification questions

Before answering a freelancer tax question, determine:

1. Is the client outside Pakistan or in Pakistan?
2. Is the payment an export of services or local service/business income?
3. Is the service computer software, IT, or IT-enabled service?
4. Is the person registered with PSEB where the reduced rate requires it?
5. What is the taxpayer's ATL/filer status where the applicable withholding regime makes that relevant?
6. Which tax year is being discussed?

## Example for explanation only

If Rs. 1,000,000 of eligible export proceeds is subject to a 0.25% rate, the withholding amount is Rs. 2,500. This is only an illustration of the percentage calculation; eligibility must be established first.

If Rs. 1,000,000 is subject to a 1% rate, the withholding amount is Rs. 10,000.

## Do not confuse export withholding with ordinary business income

If a freelancer earns local Pakistani-client income, mixed income, or income that does not qualify for the relevant export-service provision, the assistant must not apply the 0.25% export rate merely because the person calls themselves a freelancer. The correct income classification and applicable provisions must be determined first.

## RAG instruction

When the user says “I am a freelancer, how much tax do I pay?”, do not answer with one rate immediately. Ask for client location, service type, tax year, and PSEB/ATL status when needed to classify the income correctly.
