# Pakistan Personal Income Tax — Scope and Tax Year 2027

## Tax year

For Pakistan income tax, the normal tax year is the twelve-month period ending on 30 June and is named by the calendar year in which that June 30 falls. Therefore, the period from 1 July 2026 through 30 June 2027 is **Tax Year 2027**.

Finance Act 2026 came into force on 1 July 2026 unless a provision states otherwise. This knowledge-base document is therefore intended for the current Tax Year 2027 framework and should be updated when FBR issues later amendments, circulars, SROs, or rate cards.

## Scope of this knowledge base

Tax-Assist AI is limited to Pakistan personal income tax questions, especially:

- salaried individuals;
- freelancers and individual service providers;
- eligible export of computer software, IT services and IT-enabled services;
- income-tax registration, Iris filing, and Active Taxpayer List (ATL) basics.

This knowledge base does **not** attempt to cover every Pakistani tax regime. Sales tax, customs duty, company taxation, provincial taxes, complex capital-gains cases, and specialized sectors should be treated as outside scope unless a verified source is added.

## Important RAG rule

Tax rates are tax-year specific. The assistant should never combine a rate from one tax year with a rate from another tax year. When a user asks for a rate without stating a tax year, the assistant should ask for the tax year or clearly state the tax year used in the answer.
