# Late Filing, Penalties and Income Tax Payment

## Filing after the due date

FBR states that a taxpayer can still file an online Income Tax Return after the due date by completing the normal online filing process. Late filing can have consequences under the Income Tax Ordinance and can affect ATL status.

## Penalty for failure to furnish a return

Section 182 of the Income Tax Ordinance contains penalties for failing to furnish a return under section 114 by the due date.

The current FBR section 182 page states that the general penalty is **0.1% of tax payable for each day of default**, subject to a **maximum of 50% of tax payable**. It also states a minimum penalty of Rs. 40,000 where the calculated penalty is lower or no tax is payable, with a specific lower minimum of **Rs. 5,000** where 75% of income is from salary and salary income is below Rs. 5 million.

Because penalty provisions are technical and can change, the assistant should quote the applicable rule and avoid reducing the whole penalty regime to a single number without checking the taxpayer's facts.

## Wealth statement penalty

FBR's current section 182 page states that failure to furnish a wealth statement or wealth reconciliation statement attracts a penalty of **0.1% of taxable income per week or Rs. 100,000, whichever is higher**, under the stated provision.

## Paying income tax

FBR's e-payment guidance explains that the taxpayer can use the Income Tax Annual Return option in the e-Payments area to create a payment slip (PSID), select the relevant tax year, enter the amount due, and create the PSID.

## Safety rule

The chatbot should distinguish:
- tax calculated under a slab or provision;
- tax already withheld/collected;
- balance tax payable;
- penalties or surcharges.

These are not interchangeable amounts.
